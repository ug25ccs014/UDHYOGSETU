# Fire Safety No Objection Certificate (NOC)

Fire safety is governed by the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety
Measures Act, 2006 and the Maharashtra Fire Prevention and Life Safety
Measures Rules, 2009. Establishments that are high-risk occupancies, including
major industrial premises, warehouses storing flammable goods, high-rise
buildings, and hazardous process units, must obtain a No Objection Certificate
from the Chief Fire Officer before use of the premises.

## Applicability

Industrial buildings and factories with large worker headcounts, hazardous
materials storage, or multi-storey layouts are covered. Commercial buildings
above a specified height and capacity are also covered. The NOC confirms that
the building plan meets fire and life safety requirements.

## Compliance requirements

- Fire extinguishers and hydrant systems as per the approved plan
- Escape routes and illuminated exit signage
- Storage of flammable material in designated areas
- Annual testing of fire detection and suppression systems
- Trained fire wardens among employees

## Documents required

- Building plan approved by the municipal authority
- Structural drawings showing escape routes
- Certificate on installation of fire-fighting equipment
- Undertaking on compliance with fire safety norms

## Typical timeline

Fire NOC for industrial units: 15-30 days from a complete application and
successful site inspection.
