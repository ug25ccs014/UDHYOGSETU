# Factories Act Registration and Factory License

## Registration and License

Under the Factories Act, 1948, a factory is a premise where ten or more workers
work if power is used, or twenty or more workers work without power, in a
manufacturing process. The occupier must register the factory and obtain a
factory license from the Director of Industrial Safety and Health (DISH) in
Maharashtra before commencing work.

## Renewal

The factory license is issued for a period not exceeding one year and must be
renewed every year. The renewal application is submitted online along with the
prescribed fee which is based on the number of workers employed in the factory.

## Compliance requirements

Registered factories must comply with provisions on safety, welfare, and
occupational health:

- Display of abstract of the Act and standing orders
- Adequate ventilation and lighting in workrooms
- Fire safety measures and emergency exits
- Drinking water, latrines, and washing facilities
- Medical facilities for workers
- Member of worker union consultation committees where applicable

## Documents required

- Factory building plan approved by the local authority
- Site plan and layout showing machinery placement
- Details of filtering and ventilation equipment
- Worker strength and shift details

## Typical timeline

Fresh factory license: 15-30 days from complete submission.
Annual renewal: 7-15 days for renewals with no change in layout.
