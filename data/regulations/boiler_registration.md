# Boiler Registration (Maharashtra)

Every steam boiler operated in Maharashtra must be registered with the Director
of Steam Boilers before it is put into use. Registration is mandatory under the
Indian Boilers Act, 1923 and the Boiler Rules. The owner or operator submits an
application to the Director of Steam Boilers along with a certificate of
manufacture, design drawings, and hydraulic test reports.

## Applicability

A boiler is any closed vessel exceeding 22.75 litres in capacity that is used
for generating steam under pressure. Any new boiler installation, including a
second-hand boiler brought into the state, requires a fresh certificate.
Boilers must also undergo annual inspection and the registration certificate
must be renewed periodically.

## Documents required

- Certificate of manufacture from the boiler manufacturer
- Design drawings and calculations approved by the Competent Authority
- Hydraulic test certificate performed before installation
- Site inspection report
- Owner identification and proof of premises

## Typical timeline

Initial inspection and registration is normally completed within 45 to 60 days
from submission of a complete application. Annual inspection for renewal is a
condition of continued operation. Factories that install boilers should plan
for boiler inspections before starting continuous production.
