# MPCB Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate

The Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) issues Consent to Establish
(No Objection Certificate) and Consent to Operate (Renewal) under the Water
(Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 and the Air (Prevention and
Control of Pollution) Act, 1981.

## Consent to Establish

Before establishing any industry that may release water or air pollutants, the
project proponent must obtain Consent to Establish. The application describes
the process, effluent treatment plant (ETP) design, air pollution control
equipment, and the pollution potential of the industry. Consent to Establish
is valid for the construction period of the unit.

## Consent to Operate

Once the unit is commissioned, the plant must obtain Consent to Operate. This
consent is renewed annually and is subject to compliance with discharge norms
and environmental standards. Renewal applications should be submitted at least
thirty days before the expiry of the current consent.

## Pollution potential categories

Industries are categorised by the MPCB based on their pollution potential:
red (high), orange (medium), and green (low). Textile processing, chemical
units, and metal finishing are typically red category units and require a
complete ETP. Effluent quality is monitored quarterly through NOC/consent
compliance reports.

## Documents required

- Consent to Establish order
- Process flow diagram with water balance
- ETP design and mass balance
- Air pollution control equipment list
- Hazardous waste authorisation where applicable

## Typical timeline

Consent to Establish: 45-60 days for complete applications.
Consent to Operate: 30-45 days for routine renewals.
